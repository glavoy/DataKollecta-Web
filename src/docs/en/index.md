# What is DataKollecta?

DataKollecta is a platform for collecting survey and study data in the field, including places with no internet connection. It has two parts:

- **The DataKollecta website** (this portal). You use it to set up projects, build surveys, decide who has access, and view and export the data that comes back.
- **The DataKollecta field app.** Field workers run it on Android phones and tablets. It downloads your surveys, runs interviews **completely offline**, and uploads the records when a connection is available.

It is built for research projects, cross-sectional surveys, cohort studies and clinical trials. Teams use it where interviews happen far from reliable networks, and where every change to a record needs to be traceable.

## What you can do on the website

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Projects](/docs/projects/create) | A project holds everything for one study: its surveys, data, people and settings. |
| [Members](/docs/people/members) | The people who use the **website** for a project, each with a role: owner, editor or viewer. |
| [Field team](/docs/people/field-team) | The logins your data collectors type into the **field app**. |
| [Surveys](/docs/surveys/overview) | Your questionnaires, their versions, and whether each one is a draft, in testing, live, or finished. |
| [Survey Designer](/docs/designer/overview) | A built-in editor for building questionnaires with skip logic, validation, calculations and repeating sections. |
| [Data](/docs/data/browse) | Browse uploaded records and their edit history, and export to CSV. |

## Where to start

- **New to DataKollecta?** Read [How it works](/docs/how-it-works), then follow the [Quick start](/docs/quick-start).
- **Building a questionnaire?** Go to [The Survey Designer](/docs/designer/overview), or work through the [household survey tutorial](/docs/designer/tutorial).
- **Managing a team in the field?** See [Field team](/docs/people/field-team) and [The field app and sync](/docs/field-app).
- **Something not working?** Try the [FAQ and troubleshooting](/docs/faq) page.

> **Field workers:** This documentation covers the website. To learn how to run interviews on the phone, see the *DataKollecta User Guide* for the field app, which your project team can give you.
