# Signing up and your account

A website account is for people who manage studies: building surveys, managing teams and working with data. Data collectors don't need one. They use a [field-team login](/docs/people/field-team) in the app instead.

## Sign up

1. On the home page, choose **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your full name, your email address and a password. Enter the password twice.
3. Look for an email asking you to verify your address, and follow its link.
4. Come back and choose **Sign In**.

Your password must be **at least 10 characters** long. There are no other rules, such as requiring symbols or digits, so a long passphrase is a good choice.

When you sign up, you can create your own projects straight away. To join an existing project, ask its owner to [add you as a member](/docs/people/members). You won't receive an invitation email. The project simply appears in your **Projects** list.

## Sign in

Choose **Sign In** on the home page, then enter your email and password. Signing in takes you to your **Projects** list.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, type your email address.
2. Choose **Forgot password?**.
3. Open the reset email and follow its link to choose a new password.

## My Account

Choose **My Account** in the left sidebar to:

- **Update your profile**: change your full name. You can't change your email address.
- **See your projects**: every project you belong to, with your role in each.
- **Change your password**: enter the new password twice.

![My Account](/docs/img/account/my-account.jpg)

> **"Please sign in again" when changing your password?** For security, you can change your password only within 24 hours of signing in. Sign out, sign back in, and try again.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign Out** at the bottom of the left sidebar. The portal version shown underneath (for example `v1.0.0 · a1b2c3d`) tells support exactly which release you are using.
