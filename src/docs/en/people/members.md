# Members

**Members** are the people who use this **website** for a project. Each member has a role that controls what they can do. Data collectors who only use the field app are not members: they are your [field team](/docs/people/field-team).

Open the project's **Members** tab to see who has access, their role, and when they were added.

![The Members tab](/docs/img/people/members-tab.jpg)

## Roles

| Role | In short |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full control: manages members, surveys, settings and data, and can delete the project. |
| **Editor** | Builds and deploys surveys, manages the field team, and views and exports data. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only. Can view and export data. |

For the full list of who can do what, see [Roles and permissions](/docs/people/roles).

A project can have more than one owner. We recommend at least two, so that the project isn't stuck if one person leaves.

## Add a member

Only owners can add members.

1. Make sure the person has already [signed up](/docs/account) for an account. DataKollecta doesn't send invitations, so you can only add someone who already has an account.
2. On the **Members** tab, choose **Add Member**.
3. Start typing their email address or name. Matching accounts appear as you type.
4. Select the person, then choose a **Role**.
5. Choose **Add Member**.

![Adding a member: search for an existing account](/docs/img/people/add-member.jpg)

The project then appears in that person's **Projects** list the next time they load it. They don't receive an email.

## Change a member's role

Owners can change roles. Open the **⋮** menu on the member's row, choose **Change Role**, pick the new role, and choose **Save**.

> Be careful when making someone an **Owner**. Owners can remove other members, including other owners, and can delete the project.

## Remove a member

Open the **⋮** menu on the member's row and choose **Remove**. The person immediately loses access to the project and all its data. Records they created or changed keep their history.

You can't change your own role or remove yourself. Another owner has to do that.
