# Installing the field app

The DataKollecta field app runs on Android phones and tablets. It isn't in the Google Play Store yet, so you install it directly from its download link.

**→ [Download the DataKollecta app (APK)](https://github.com/glavoy/DataKollecta/releases/latest/download/datakollecta.apk)**

This link always downloads the newest version. Earlier versions and release notes are on the [releases page](https://github.com/glavoy/DataKollecta/releases).

## Install it

1. On the phone or tablet, open the download link above in the browser (for example Chrome). The file is called `datakollecta.apk` and is about 65 MB, so Wi-Fi is best.
2. When the download finishes, tap it to open it.
3. Android asks whether to allow your browser to **install unknown apps**. Choose **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**.
5. **Google Play Protect** may say it doesn't recognise the app. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store. Choose **More details → Install anyway**.
6. Open **DataKollecta** from the app list.

You only need to allow unknown apps once per browser. You can turn the setting off again after installing.

## Sign in

In the app's **Settings**, add your project with the **project code**, **username** and **password** your project gave you. Then download your surveys. See [The field app and sync](/docs/field-app).

## Updating to a new version

Download and install from the same link. The new version installs **over** the old one, and your surveys, saved records and settings are kept, including records that haven't been synced yet.

> **Don't uninstall the app to update it.** Uninstalling deletes everything stored in the app, including any records that haven't been synced. Sync before doing anything unusual with the app.

## Requirements

- An Android phone or tablet. iPhones and iPads aren't supported.
- Enough free storage for the app and your data. Allow at least 200 MB.
- An internet connection to sign in, download surveys and sync. Interviews themselves work offline.
